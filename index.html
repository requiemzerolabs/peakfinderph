Privacy Policy for PeakFinder PH

Application: PeakFinder PH (Package: ph.peakfinder.app)
Effective Date: November 10, 2026
Last Updated: November 10, 2026

🔒 Privacy Summary (Offline-First App)

PeakFinder PH is designed with an offline-first architecture. We do not sell, rent, or transmit your personal mountaineering profile, summit logs, or hiking photos to remote third-party servers. Your data is stored locally on your own device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the PeakFinder PH mobile application for Android, available on the Google Play Store, and related web interfaces. This document describes how your information is handled, what permissions the app requests, and how you maintain total control over your data.

2. Information We Collect and Store

PeakFinder PH operates locally on your device without requiring you to create an external cloud account. The data you enter into the application includes:

  • Mountaineer Profile: Your chosen display name, moniker / trail name, home location, and optional profile photo.
  • Summit Hike Logs: Mountain summits logged, dates climbed, trail routes taken, hike duration, weather notes, companions, and guide names.
  • Trail Memories & Photos: Photos you choose to upload as summit memories for your digital mountaineer passport or conquered album. These photos are compressed and stored locally on your device via IndexedDB (Local Storage Manager).
  • Hiking Wishlist: Mountains you bookmark to hike in the future.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

PeakFinder PH requests only the permissions necessary to provide hiking navigation, certificate generation, and photo uploads:

Permission Platform / Version Purpose
READ_MEDIA_IMAGES Android 13+ (API 33+) Allows you to select trail photos from your device gallery to attach to summit logs and profile photos.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Android 12 and older (API ≤ 32) Required on legacy Android versions to save generated Summit Certificates, digital Passport stamps, and backup files to your device storage.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Android 13+ (API 33+) Allows the app to notify you when a certificate or passport JPEG download is complete or when a summit milestone badge is achieved.
INTERNET All Versions Used exclusively to fetch open-source public map tiles (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, ESRI World Imagery) and launch Google Maps / Waze trail navigation links.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

PeakFinder PH utilizes standard open-source map tile providers and navigation utilities:

  • OpenStreetMap & OpenTopoMap: Used to display topographic elevation contours and street base maps. Accessing map tiles is subject to standard OpenStreetMap Foundation guidelines.
  • Google Maps & Waze: When you tap "Open in Google Maps" or "Waze", the app opens your external mapping application with the trail jump-off coordinates. No personal identity is shared with these services by PeakFinder PH.

PeakFinder PH does not include third-party advertising SDKs (such as AdMob), social media trackers, or analytics telemetry that track you across applications.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you have immediate and permanent control over it:

  • Exporting Data: You can export a full JSON backup of all your hike logs, achievements, and wishlist at any time from the Profile page.
  • Deleting Specific Logs: You can remove any individual mountain log or summit photo directly from the Summits & Badges tab.
  • Complete Data Wipe: Under Profile → Local Storage Manager, you can click "Delete Profile & Wipe All PeakFinder Data" to permanently erase all records, photos, and cached database stores.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling the PeakFinder PH application automatically deletes all locally stored app data from your Android device.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PeakFinder PH is intended for outdoor enthusiasts, hikers, and mountaineers of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. All user inputs remain strictly on the local device.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and made accessible through the app store listing.
